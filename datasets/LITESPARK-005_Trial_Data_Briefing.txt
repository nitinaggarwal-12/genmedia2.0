GLOBAL PHARMA CLINICAL TRIAL BRIEFING & TELEMETRY MANIFEST
STUDY PROTOCOL: LITESPARK-005 (NCT04195750)
THERAPEUTIC AREA: Oncology / Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma (RCC)
INVESTIGATOR SUMMARY: HIF-2α Inhibition in Pretreated Advanced RCC

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STUDY DESIGN
LITESPARK-005 is a randomized, open-label Phase III study evaluating Product-C (compound_gamma), a first-in-class HIF-2α inhibitor, versus everolimus in patients with advanced RCC who progressed after immune checkpoint and anti-angiogenic therapies.

2. KEY EFFICACY ENDPOINTS
- Primary Endpoint (PFS): Statistically significant reduction in risk of disease progression or death (HR 0.75; 95% CI: 0.63, 0.90; p=0.0008).
- Key Secondary Endpoint (ORR): Confirmed Objective Response Rate of 22% in the Product-C arm vs 3.5% in the control arm.
- Duration of Response (DoR): Median DoR was not reached in the Product-C arm, with 67% of responders maintaining response at 12 months.

3. SAFETY REGISTRY SUMMARY (VAULT REF #V-2026-WR005)
- Overall Tolerability: Treatment-related Grade 3/4 Adverse Events occurred in 30% of patients.
- Most Frequent Adverse Reactions: Anemia (severe, requiring erythropoiesis-stimulating agents or transfusion) and hypoxia (requiring oxygen therapy).
- Discontinuation Rate: Only 6% of patients discontinued Product-C due to adverse reactions, highlighting long-term outpatient tolerability.
