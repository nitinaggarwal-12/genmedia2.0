GLOBAL PHARMA ENTERPRISE LLC - FDA APPROVED PRESCRIBING LABEL 2026
PRODUCT-C (compound_gamma) tablets, for oral use
Initial U.S. Approval: 2021 | Indication: Advanced Renal Cell Carcinoma (LITESPARK-005 Study)

1. INDICATIONS AND USAGE
Product-C is a hypoxia-inducible factor-2 alpha (HIF-2α) inhibitor indicated for the treatment of adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) following progression on a programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1) or programmed death-ligand 1 (PD-L1) inhibitor and a vascular endothelial growth factor tyrosine kinase inhibitor (VEGF-TKI).

2. CLINICAL TRIAL RESULTS (LITESPARK-005, NCT04195750)
In the pivotal Phase III LITESPARK-005 clinical study (N=746), Product-C demonstrated superior Progression-Free Survival (PFS) and Objective Response Rate compared to everolimus.
- Objective Response Rate (ORR): 22% (95% CI: 18, 27) for Product-C compared to 3.5% for everolimus (p<0.00001).
- Durable tumor regression and vessel reduction observed across pretreated clinical cohorts.

3. WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (MATERIAL REVIEW SAFETY VAULT REF #V-2026-WR005)
- Anemia & Hypoxia: Grade 3/4 adverse events occurred in 30% of patients, primarily manifesting as severe anemia requiring transfusion or hypoxia requiring supplemental oxygen.
- Monitor hemoglobin and pulse oximetry levels prior to initiation of Product-C and periodically throughout treatment.
- Embryo-Fetal Toxicity: Can cause fetal harm. Advise females of reproductive potential of potential risk and to use effective contraception.

4. REGULATORY GOVERNANCE & FORM FDA 2253 COMPLIANCE
Promotional assets referencing LITESPARK-005 efficacy (22% ORR) must explicitly pair claims with safety disclosures regarding Grade 3/4 anemia and hypoxia under Form FDA 2253 requirements.
